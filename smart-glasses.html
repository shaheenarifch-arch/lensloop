What they actually do

Three core features show up across the line: a built-in camera for hands-free photos and short video, a voice assistant for basic queries, and Bluetooth for phone calls and audio.

Who they're worth it for

Best fit if you already wear glasses daily and want to cut down on carrying a separate pair of earbuds, or you want a low-effort way to record point-of-view video.

Battery

Expect a single-day cycle

Camera and Bluetooth features draw noticeably more power than a plain frame. Treat these like another device that needs a nightly charge.

Lens options

Optical vs. photochromic vs. standard

The optical variants take a prescription lens like a normal pair of glasses. Photochromic versions darken in sunlight and functionally double as sunglasses.

Privacy

Recording courtesy

A camera in a frame that looks like ordinary glasses means people around you often don't know it's recording. Worth a quick check of local recording-consent norms before using them.
