Budget Eyewear Showdown: Three Top Online Retailers Compared

Updated September 2026 · 9 min read

If you are shopping for prescription glasses online, three names keep coming up: Eydology, Warby Parker, and Zenni Optical. Each takes a different approach to making eyewear affordable, and the best choice depends on your priorities. We bought frames from all three and compared them across every dimension that matters.

Price Comparison at a Glance

Let us start with what most people care about most: cost.

  • Zenni Optical: Frames from $6.95. Single-vision lenses included at base price. AR coating adds $5-8. Total for a basic pair: $12-25.
  • Eydology: Frames with lenses from ~$25. AR coating included free. Total for a well-equipped pair: $25-45. See current prices.
  • Warby Parker: Frames from $95 with single-vision lenses. AR and scratch-resistant coatings included. Progressive lenses add $100.

On raw price, Zenni wins. But once you add necessary coatings, the gap narrows considerably. Eydology hits a sweet spot for buyers who want a complete pair without nickel-and-diming through add-ons.

Frame Quality and Selection

Zenni

Zenni's catalog is enormous, with over 3,000 frames. Styles range from classic rectangles to bold cat-eyes to sport wraparounds. The downside of this breadth is inconsistency: some frames feel solid and well-made, while others at the low end feel flimsy. The hinge quality on sub-$15 frames can be hit-or-miss. However, their mid-range frames ($20-40) generally hold up well for daily use.

Eydology

Eydology takes a curated approach with a smaller but carefully selected catalog. Frames lean toward clean, modern aesthetics. Build quality is consistent across price points because they do not chase the absolute bottom of the market. The acetate frames feel premium for their price, and the hinge mechanisms are sturdy. If you prefer quality over quantity in selection, this is a strength.

Warby Parker

Warby Parker frames are genuinely well-made. The acetate is thick and lustrous, hinges are tight, and the finishing is excellent. Their design language is cohesive and trendy. The trade-off is a smaller selection compared to Zenni, and every frame is in the same $95-195 price band. You also get a home try-on option where five frames arrive for free to test before buying.

Lens Options

All three retailers offer standard single-vision, bifocal, and progressive lenses. Here is where they diverge:

  • Blue-light filtering: All three offer it. Zenni charges $17+, Eydology includes a blue-light option at modest cost, and Warby Parker charges $50.
  • High-index lenses: Important for strong prescriptions. Zenni offers 1.67 index from $25. Eydology and Warby Parker both offer high-index at varying upcharges.
  • Photochromic (transition) lenses: Zenni offers these from $30. Warby Parker charges $100. Eydology falls in between.
  • Progressives: Zenni starts at $35 for basic progressives. Warby Parker charges $195 total. Check our progressive vs bifocal guide if you are deciding between the two.

Shopping Experience

Warby Parker has the most polished website and app, with an excellent virtual try-on feature that uses your phone's camera. Their customer service is responsive via chat, email, and phone. Eydology's site is clean and easy to navigate, with a solid virtual try-on tool. Zenni's site is functional but can feel overwhelming due to the sheer number of options. Their filtering tools help, but browsing 3,000+ frames is a commitment.

Shipping and Returns

  • Zenni: Standard shipping takes 7-14 business days. Returns within 30 days but with conditions. International shipping available.
  • Eydology: Typically ships within 5-10 business days. 14-day return window. Check shipping details.
  • Warby Parker: Ships in 5-7 business days. Free 30-day returns with prepaid label. The gold standard for hassle-free returns.

Our Verdict

Choose Zenni if you want the absolute lowest price and don't mind spending time browsing a huge catalog. Great for buying multiple pairs cheaply.

Choose Eydology if you want solid quality and included coatings without decision fatigue. A balanced choice for most buyers. Check out the full eyeglasses collection.

Choose Warby Parker if you prioritize build quality, a premium unboxing experience, and easy returns. Worth the higher price for your primary everyday pair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is cheaper, Zenni or Eydology?
Zenni has the lowest starting price at $6.95 for frames, but Eydology includes AR coating free, making total cost similar for a fully-featured pair around $25-40.
Does Warby Parker have an online option?
Yes. Warby Parker sells online with a free home try-on program. Frames start at $95 with single-vision lenses included.
Which store has the best return policy?
Warby Parker offers 30-day free returns with prepaid shipping. Eydology allows 14-day returns. Zenni has a 30-day exchange policy but charges a restocking fee on some orders.
Can I use insurance at these online stores?
Warby Parker accepts most vision insurance plans. Zenni and Eydology accept FSA/HSA cards and provide itemized receipts for out-of-network reimbursement.
