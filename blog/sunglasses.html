Best Polarized Prescription Sunglasses Online — cheat sheet for cheap sunwear.

01

Prescription sunglasses

A dedicated frame with your prescription ground into a tinted lens. The right call if you're outdoors often and don't want to juggle two pairs.

02

Non-prescription sunglasses

Fashion or general-use sun with no correction. The cheapest way in if your vision needs are mild or these are a backup pair.

03

Clip-on sunglasses

A tinted lens that clips over your existing prescription frame. The cheapest option overall, and the only one that requires zero new fitting.

04

Sports sunglasses

Wraparound or semi-rimless shapes built for movement, usually with polarized or mirrored lens options.

Polarized vs. not

Polarized lenses cut glare from water, roads, and snow, which matters for driving and being outdoors near reflective surfaces.

A quick way to decide

Already own prescription glasses you like the fit of? Start with a clip-on. Buying your first pair of glasses altogether? A single pair of prescription sunglasses covers you without an extra buy.
