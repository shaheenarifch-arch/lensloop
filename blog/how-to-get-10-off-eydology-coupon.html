Newsletter Sign-Up Discount

Most online eyewear retailers offer a first-order discount when you sign up for their email newsletter. Eydology typically provides $10 off your first pair through their newsletter sign-up. This is the easiest and most reliable way to save. Visit the store to sign up.

Seasonal Sales

Online eyewear retailers run predictable sales throughout the year:

  • Back to school (August-September): Discounts on kids frames and bundle deals for families. Great time to stock up on school-year glasses.
  • Black Friday / Cyber Monday (November): The biggest sales of the year. Expect 20-30% off sitewide or significant BOGO deals.
  • Holiday season (December): Extended Cyber Monday deals often run through mid-December. Good for gift purchases.
  • New Year / Presidents Day (January-February): New year, new look promotions. Moderate discounts of 10-20%.
  • Memorial Day (May): Summer prep sales, especially on sunglasses and photochromic lenses.

Money-Saving Strategies Beyond Coupons

Buy Two Pairs

This sounds counterintuitive as a money-saving tip, but buying two pairs at once is often cheaper than buying one now and another later. Many retailers offer BOGO (buy one, get one) deals or steep discounts on a second pair. Having a backup pair also saves you from emergency purchases if your main pair breaks.

Skip Unnecessary Add-Ons

Not every coating and upgrade is worth the money. Here is what to keep and what to skip:

  • Keep: Anti-reflective coating (essential for daily wear), UV protection (usually free).
  • Consider: Blue-light filtering (if you have sleep issues), scratch-resistant coating (for active lifestyles).
  • Skip: Premium brand-name lens coatings that cost $50+ more than standard versions. The generic versions from online retailers are typically manufactured by the same labs.

Use FSA/HSA Funds

If you have a Flexible Spending Account (FSA) or Health Savings Account (HSA), prescription eyewear is an eligible expense. This means you are paying with pre-tax dollars, effectively saving 20-30% depending on your tax bracket. Most online retailers accept FSA/HSA debit cards at checkout and provide itemized receipts for reimbursement.

Check Your Vision Insurance

Even if an online retailer is out of network for your vision insurance, you may be able to submit receipts for out-of-network reimbursement. Many vision plans reimburse $50-150 for frames and a similar amount for lenses. The reimbursement combined with online prices often means your glasses are nearly free.

Time Your Purchase

If your current glasses are still functional, wait for a major sale. The difference between full price and a Black Friday deal can be 20-30%, which on a $50 pair saves you $10-15, and on prescription sunglasses or progressives can save $30-60.
